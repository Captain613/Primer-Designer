设计：深蓝与青绿色圆角底板、DNA 双螺旋、两个相向引物箭头；红色碱基表现 SNP。图标不含文字，适合桌面缩略显示。

文件：`PrimerDesigner-v1.png`（原图）、`PrimerDesigner-v1.ico`（桌面快捷方式使用）。

Flat vector-like precision with only a subtle navy-to-teal background gradient, no photorealism, no extruded 3D, no drop shadow outside the tile, no glass effects, no mockup, no frame, no UI screenshot, no watermark, no letters, no labels, no extra objects. Render the finished icon asset only, centered, crisp, balanced and professional. Preserve genuine transparent alpha outside the rounded square.
